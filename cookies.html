Cookie Notice

Last updated: 4 November 2026

1. What this notice covers

This notice explains how the website at medullahr.com and www.medullahr.com (the "Website"), operated by JOLAG Consulting FZE, uses cookies and similar technologies such as browser storage. It should be read with our Privacy Policy.

2. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device. Similar technologies, such as local storage, do the same job. They can make a site work, keep it secure, remember choices, or measure how it is used.

3. What we use

We do not use advertising cookies, and we do not use cookies to track you across other websites or to build a marketing profile of you.

Type Provider Purpose
Strictly necessary and security Our hosting and security provider Protects the Website against attacks and abuse and helps it load reliably
Bot detection Our bot-detection service Checks that a person, not a robot, is submitting the demo request form. It may use technical signals or short-lived browser storage
Visit statistics Our analytics service Counts visits and pages viewed. It is designed not to use cookies and not to track individual visitors across sites

Because we only use technologies that are necessary for the Website to work securely, or that do not use cookies, we do not show a cookie consent banner.

4. Fonts

The Website loads fonts from a third-party font service. When your browser loads them, your IP address is sent to that service so the font files can be delivered. It does not place cookies on your device for this purpose.

5. Managing cookies

You can control or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking all cookies may stop some features, such as the bot-detection check on the demo request form, from working. If the form does not work, you can email us at info@medullahr.com instead.

6. Changes

We may update this notice, and will change the "Last updated" date when we do. If we add services that use other cookies we will update this notice and, where required, ask for your consent.
