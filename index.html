The problem

HR by spreadsheet and WhatsApp works — until your team grows.

For companies with 10 to 200 staff in the UAE, manual HR quietly turns into hours of hunting through chat threads, updating separate trackers, and hoping no visa renewal date slips through.

Spreadsheets
Staff_Leave_Master_FINAL_v4.xlsx Three versions saved across different laptops

Spreadsheets that never quite match

One sheet tracks annual leave, another tracks payroll allowances, and a third lists visa expiry dates. One manual edit puts the whole record out of sync.

WhatsApp messages
"Hi, how many leave days do I have left?" Sent in chat threads with no formal record

Approvals buried in WhatsApp chats

Staff ask for sick days, annual leave, and salary certificates in chat messages. Approvals get forgotten between meetings and never make it to payroll on time.

Chasing emails
Re: Fwd: Passport & Emirates ID scan Attachments scattered across inboxes

Chasing emails for documents and renewals

Signed contracts, passport scans, Emirates IDs, and residency visas sit across separate email threads — making renewals and handovers stressful.

MedullaHR replaces the patchwork with one calm, structured system — where you switch on only the modules your company needs.
