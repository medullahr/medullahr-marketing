The problem

HR by spreadsheet is unnecessary hard work.

HR running on manual processes, spreadsheets, calls, email threads, scattered trackers and a good memory, hoping no visa or renewal date slips through. That's avoidable.

Spreadsheets

Spreadsheets that never quite match

One sheet tracks annual leave, another tracks payroll allowances, and a third lists visa expiry dates. One manual edit puts the whole record out of sync.

Letters

Salary certificates, NOC letters and experience letters get typed up one at a time. Every request takes someone's afternoon.

Chasing emails

Signed contracts, passport scans, Emirates IDs, and residency visas sit across separate email threads, making renewals and handovers stressful.
